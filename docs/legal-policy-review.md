Prepared November 20, 2026. The policies are staged, not legal approval or proof that live transactions work. No live account, upload, charge, refund, provider write or support message was created for this audit. Review the gates below before merging to an automatically deployed production branch or submitting the website for Dodo verification.

## Dodo verification and consumer-rights research

- [Dodo Account Verification](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/verification-process) calls for a live website with matching product details, pricing, Terms, Privacy, Refund and Cancellation and a monitored contact route. [Merchant Acceptance](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/merchant-acceptance) also expects clearly described, usable digital products. **Public policies alone do not make an unavailable paid generation workflow eligible.** Resolve the gap between marketing claims, actual checkout enablement and usable delivery before accepting payment or submitting for verification.
- [Dodo Buyer Terms](https://dodopayments.com/legal/buyer-terms) describe the reseller/transaction relationship; [Dodo Privacy Policy](https://dodopayments.com/legal/privacy-policy) and [Dodo DPA](https://dodopayments.com/legal/data-processing-agreement) distinguish its independent controller role for MoR activities from separate processor tasks. [Dodo Refunds](https://docs.dodopayments.com/features/transactions/refunds) describes ordinary provider request and processing limits and original-payment-method refunds. Do not copy operational limits into a provision cutting off mandatory rights. Validate an escalation path with Dodo for lawful remedies outside the ordinary process. [Dodo FAQs](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/faq) confirm that a no-refund policy cannot stop valid card disputes.
- **India** (an explicit pricing market, not proof of entity jurisdiction): [Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/consumer-protection-acts) address digital products, disclosures, grievance handling and remedies for deficient/nonconforming service. Confirm the roles of the merchant and MoR and publish required identity/address/officer details. Review phased commencement of the [final DPDP Rules, 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) with counsel.
- **Potential non-INR markets**, not a verified sales territory list: the [EU guidance](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ%3AC%3A2021%3A525%3AFULL) distinguishes digital services from online digital content and specifies when cancellation rights can be lost; [UK distance selling](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses/online-selling) likewise requires specific disclosures/consent for applicable digital supplies. Do not treat wallet credit delivery or a link to these policies as a consent to lose withdrawal rights. [US CFPB billing error guidance](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/13/) covers certain card disputes, including non-delivery; US state consumer/privacy laws still require a market-specific review. [Australian ACCC guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/problem-with-a-product-or-service-you-bought/repair-replace-refund-cancel) distinguishes change of mind from remedies for failures of consumer guarantees. [UK ICO transparency guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-be-informed/) outlines disclosures such as identity, bases, recipients, retention and transfers.

## Owner and counsel review — required before publication

1. Provide the verified legal name of the individual proprietor or entity exactly as on the Dodo supplier account, registration or trading details where required, principal/registered business address, and governing jurisdiction. Confirm whether to designate any forum or leave nonexclusive applicable law in place. No entity, address or forum has been guessed or left as a public placeholder.
2. Confirm the markets where Zybble actually offers and accepts purchases (the USD fallback is not a country list). Counsel must determine which jurisdictions apply and review digital-service and credit classification, withdrawal periods, mandatory refunds, pre-contract notice/confirmation and any required consent flow. Review the 18+/legal-capacity rule, warranty/liability wording and treatment of paid balances at suspension/closure. This is not a full market-by-market legal sign-off; the market and operator inputs are missing.
3. Confirm the published support email is monitored and approved for complaints and privacy requests; provide formal grievance officer, privacy/data-protection contact and regional representative details where required. Confirm applicable complaint-handling timeframes and that the team can meet them. Confirm contact-form delivery in production or keep the direct email route visible.
4. Verify hosted provider settings (live Dodo approval/readiness, Supabase mail and log settings, enabled R2 buckets and cleanup, processor contracts, retention/deletion and transfer safeguards). Confirm account/media deletion request operations and provider roles/locations, privacy rights workflow, and whether additional notices or choices are required. Do not activate new ClipSDK processing or make data-use or deletion promises without verification.
5. Confirm the production marketing and checkout states match the currently usable product. Do not submit an unavailable paid product for verification. After owner/counsel sign-off and deployment, run `ORIGIN=https://zybble.com node scripts/qa/legal.mjs` with the browser tooling available; it checks public routes, real footer links, indexability and sitemap. Local build checks do not prove the deployed site has changed.
